Five crepes worth knowing

Crepe de chine

A lightweight, plain-weave fabric traditionally made from silk with tightly twisted yarns. It has a soft, matte surface with the faintest pebbly texture and a fluid drape that skims the body.

Georgette

Sheer and springy with a slightly grainy feel, created by alternating tightly twisted yarns. Georgette floats and flutters, making it a favourite for layered skirts and soft sleeves.

A knitted crepe with a fine textured surface and natural stretch. It is comfortable, resists creasing and is a dream for travel, wrap dresses and easy trousers.

Wool crepe

Wool woven with high-twist yarns for a dry, slightly grainy handle. It holds tailoring beautifully while still moving softly, and it breathes and resists wrinkles.

Care

Keep crepe looking new

Textured fabrics reward gentle handling. Four simple habits protect their drape and colour.

01

Read the label. Silk and viscose crepes often need cool hand washing or dry cleaning.

02

Wash cool and gently. Heat and agitation can shrink crepe and flatten its texture.

03

Never wring. Roll pieces in a towel to remove water, then dry flat or on a padded hanger.

04

Steam, don’t press. A steamer relaxes creases without crushing the surface.

Colours borrowed from the Alps

Ask the editors
What is crepe fabric?

Crepe is a family of fabrics with a crinkled, grainy or pebbled surface. The texture usually comes from very tightly twisted yarns, a special weave or a finishing process. Crepe can be made from silk, wool, polyester, viscose and other fibres.

What is the difference between crepe and crepon?

Crepon is a heavier, more strongly textured member of the crepe family, with crinkles or ridges that typically run lengthwise. Lighter crepes such as crepe de chine have a much subtler surface.

Does crepe wrinkle easily?

Most crepes resist wrinkling better than smooth fabrics because their textured surface hides small creases. Crepe jersey and crepon are particularly low-maintenance, which makes them excellent for travel.

Can crepe shrink when washed?

Yes, some crepes can shrink or lose their texture with heat and agitation, especially silk and viscose versions. Always follow the care label; many crepes need a cool hand wash or dry cleaning.

What does alpine style mean?

To us, alpine style blends practical mountain layers with refined fabrics: cosy knits, tailored coats and fluid crepe pieces in a palette inspired by snow, stone, pine and wildflowers.
