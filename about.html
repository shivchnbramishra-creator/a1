About Crepon Alpine

An independent fashion guide for people who care about how clothes feel, move and last, with a soft spot for mountain landscapes.

Our story

A crinkled dress and a mountain holiday

Crepon Alpine began with a summer dress that survived a week in a backpack, a long train ride and a dinner in a mountain village without a single crease. It was made of crepon, and it sparked a fascination with the whole crepe family.

We started learning how fabrics are made, why some drape and others don’t, and how to dress well for real life in changeable weather. This website shares those lessons.

Independent

We do not sell clothing and are not affiliated with any brand, retailer or designer.

Fabric first

We focus on what clothes are made of and how they behave, not on logos or trends.

Made to last

We encourage buying thoughtfully, caring gently and wearing favourite pieces for years.

How we work

Research and real wear

Our guides draw on textile references, fibre and care-labelling information, and conversations with dressmakers and people who wear these fabrics every day. We explain what fabrics generally do rather than ranking products.
